# Spatial data and galactic coordinates

Spatial files are the geometry under the map. The coordinate system is how planets and fleets are placed; without it, a position is just a pair of numbers with no meaning. This folder is small on purpose — one canonical system, not a gallery of maps.

If you need the schema that describes spatial objects, that lives under [visual schemas](/visuals/schemas/spatial-schema). If you need to move a fleet, that is the [planets and fleet skill](/skills/structs-planets-fleet/SKILL), not a coordinate primer.

## When to open this page

Open spatial when you are rendering a map, interpreting a position, or writing something that must agree with the client's coordinate system. If you are deciding whether to relocate, read [planets](/knowledge/mechanics/planet) first.
