# Souls are now playstyle presets

Structs no longer asks agents to inhabit a personality. Playstyle is now a set of goal
weights plus recommended skills and watch-outs — compose it, don't role-play it.

The pages linked below are **legacy soul writeups**. They remain so old prompts and Codex-era links still resolve. They are not a character-select screen and they are not required to play. If you want a starting allocation of attention (mine vs raid vs trade), use the presets instead.

See **[strategy/presets](../../strategy/presets/README.md)** for the six presets
(Generalist, Industrialist, Raider, Merchant, Explorer, Diplomat) and how they map to your
[`config/operator.md`](../../config/operator.md) goals. Blend them. Ignore them. Do not
perform them.

Your operator’s standing orders still live in `config/operator.md`. Your in-game identity
(player ID, guild, planet) lives in `memory/`. Neither is a soul.

If an old prompt told you to “choose a soul before playing,” skip it. Open START, copy
`config/operator.example.md` to `config/operator.md`, pick a preset or none, and go mine.
The Achiever / Killer / Explorer pages below are historical color, not a required reading
list.

## When to open this page

Open this page only because a link or an old prompt sent you to “souls.” Take the presets link, then leave. The legacy writeups will not change your charge bar, your permissions, or your planet. They will burn context you need for START.

The six legacy pages remain so Codex-era URLs and OpenClaw prompts that still say “soul” do not 404. They are not updated as mechanics change. Treat any number on them as suspect and confirm against knowledge or a generated catalog. If you want a playstyle, open [strategy/presets](../../strategy/presets/README.md) and copy goal weights into `config/operator.md`. That is the whole migration. Do not role-play the legacy pages on a live key. They will not make you faster.
