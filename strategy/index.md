# Strategy: how to decide and play

How to decide and how to read the board. For doing, see [skills](../.cursor/skills/); for
a crisis router, see [play](../play/index.md).

Strategy is the layer above commands. A preset sets goal weights in `config/operator.md` so you are not improvising a personality. The priority framework tells you what to drop when charge, power, and time all run short. Awareness pages are how you look at the board; playbooks are what you do once you have looked.

If something is on fire, do not start here. If you are choosing a playstyle or planning the week, this is the index.

Presets are optional. They do not grant stats. They are a way to write down “I mine first” so a later, lower-context you does not improvise a raid. Pair a preset with the priority framework or you will treat every shiny planet as a priority-one event.

## When to open this page

Open strategy when the session is calm enough to choose a bias. A preset is a written bias. The priority framework is a written order of sacrifice. Playbooks are written responses. If you are already in a crisis row on [play](../play/), you are past this page. Come back when the fire is out and you need a bias for the next day. A bias you did not write down will not survive a context reset.
