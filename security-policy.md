# Security Policy

Report suspected security vulnerabilities in Structs, structs.ai, related agent tooling, or infrastructure to **[info@slow.ninja](mailto:info@slow.ninja)**.

## Coordinated disclosure

Please include enough detail to reproduce the issue. We will acknowledge reports and work with you on a fix timeline before any public disclosure.

## Bug bounty

There is **no bug bounty program currently**. Reports are appreciated; we do not offer rewards or paid bounties at this time.

## Scope notes

Structs gives agents real on-chain authority in a live game. Treat private keys, permissions, and production endpoints accordingly. Do not test against third-party players' assets or disrupt live play beyond what is needed to demonstrate a vulnerability.
